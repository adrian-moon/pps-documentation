VII. SAFETY & WORK AREA POLICY

Workplace Safety Rules​

Your safety is the constant concern of this organization. Every precaution has been taken to provide a safe workplace. We take your safety seriously and any willful or habitual violation of safety rules will be considered cause for dismissal. Pressure Profile Systems, Inc. is sincerely concerned for the health and well being of each employee. The cooperation of every staff member is necessary to make this entity a safe place in which to work. Help yourself and others by immediately reporting unsafe conditions or hazards to your supervisor. Give earnest consideration to the rules of safety presented to you published in the PPS 2005 handbook. Begin right by always thinking of safety as you perform your job, or as you learn a new one.

Safety Rules and Guidelines​

To ensure your safety, and that of your co-workers, please observe and obey the rules and guidelines appropriate to the general populace or specific jobs:

  • Observe and practice the safety procedures established for the job.
  • In case of sickness or injury, no matter how slight, report at once to your supervisor. In no case should an employee treat his or her own or someone else’s injuries or attempt to remove foreign particles from someone else’s eye.
  • In case of injury resulting in possible fracture to legs, back, or neck, or any accident resulting in an unconscious condition, or a severe head injury, the employee is not to be moved until medical attention has been given by authorized personnel.
  • Do not wear loose clothing or jewelry around machinery. It may catch on moving equipment and cause a serious injury.
  • The wearing of safety glasses by all employees mandatory when using hand drill and cutting tools. Strict adherence to this policy can significantly reduce the risk of eye injuries.
  • Never distract the attention of another person, as you might cause him or her to be injured. If necessary to get the attention of another person, wait until it can be done safely.
  • Where required, you must wear protective equipment, such as goggles, safety glasses, masks, gloves, hair nets, etc. appropriate to the task.
  • Keep your work area clean.
  • Use compressed air only for the job for which it is intended. Do not clean your clothes with it, and do not fool around with it.
  • Observe “No Smoking” regulations.
  • Shut down powered equipment cleaning, repairing, or leaving it.
  • Do not exceed a speed that is safe for existing conditions.
  • Running and horseplay are strictly forbidden.
  • Do not block access to fire extinguishers.
  • Do not tamper with electric controls or switches.
  • Do not operate equipment until you have been properly instructed and authorized to do so by your supervisor.
  • Do not engage in such other practices as may be inconsistent with ordinary and reasonable common sense safety rules.
  • Report any unsafe condition or acts to your supervisor.
  • Help to prevent accidents.
  • Lift properly—use your leg muscles, not your back muscles. For heavier loads, ask for assistance.
  • Do not throw objects.
  • Clean up any and all spilled liquid immediately.
  • Wear hard-sole shoes and appropriate clothing (i.e., sandals, shorts or mini dresses are not permitted).
  • Place trash and paper in proper containers and not in cans provided for cigarette butts.

Safety Checklist​

It is every employee’s responsibility to be on the lookout for possible hazards. If you spot one of the conditions on the following list—or any other possible hazardous situation— report it to your supervisor immediately.

  • Slippery floors.
  • Tripping hazards, such as sensor wiring, piping, etc.
  • Missing (or inoperative) entrance and exit signs and lighting
  • Poorly lighted stairs (Know where the emergency stairwell is located) - Loose handrails or guard rails
  • Open, loose or broken windows
  • Dangerously piled supplies or equipment
  • Electrical equipment left operating
  • Leaks of steam, water, oil, other liquids
  • Blocked aisles
  • Blocked fire extinguishers, hose sprinkler heads
  • Blocked fire doors
  • Evidence of any equipment running hot or overheating
  • Evidence of smoking in non-smoking areas
  • Safety devices not operating properly

Accident reporting​

Any injury at work—no matter how small—must be reported immediately to your supervisor and receive first-aid attention. Serious conditions often arise from small injuries if they are not cared for at once.

Work Areas​

Stock Room​

To request Parts & Material from the Stock Room you should contact the Inventory Control Specialist as this is a MANDATORY rule to control inventory and material cost as well as controlling the quality of our products. The Inventory Control Specialist is responsible for ordering and issuing parts. With this method enforced, PPS will be able to restock parts before they run out, and thus minimizing rush orders with costly shipping charges and avoiding potential delay in the delivery of our products. It is Important to remember that we many parts that are sensitive to ESD (Elector Static Discharge) damage and/or moisture in our inventory, if working with these parts it is important to remember to ground yourself and always return a ESD part back to its original package when not in use.

Labs (Test, Assembly and Development)​

These Labs are to be clean and clutter free of debris from the floor and benches. Tools should always be put back in their respective place when finished and there is to be no food allowed in these labs at any time due to our ISO & FDA compliance guidelines. Engineers are not allowed to use the Assembly Lab.

Conference Room, Cubicles & Offices​

These rooms are to be clean and picked up all times. When using the Conference Room always clean up any mess and put chairs back in an orderly manner, turn the TV monitor off after use and close applications on the computer and be sure to leave the Skype Application running. Personal Offices and Cubicles should also be kept clean and clutter free as well.

Kitchen​

The Kitchen is to be clean and orderly and no dirty items are to be left in the Kitchen sink and all trash should be thrown away and please throw out old food that has been in the refrigerator too long so as not to become smelly. Lastly the Coffee/Espresso Machine is a benefit to the employees here at PPS and thus it should always be kept clean after each use.

Receptionist, Shipping Area, IT Room and Common Walk Ways​

The Receptionist area is for Clients and Customers who visit PPS and should be kept clean and orderly at all times.

The Shipping Area is to be kept clean and orderly at all times with trash thrown away and items not left on the floor.

IT Room is home to the Laser Printer and 3D Printer and this area should be kept clean at all times, when using the Laser Printer be sure to turn it off when it is not in use and to throw away remnants from the job that was performed as well. The IT equipment and Phone System and computers should ONLY be accessed by the IT Administrator. In this area there is to be no food or drinks allowed.

Common Walk Ways should be used with respect at all times by not talking loud and being careful so as not interrupt employees working in their office or cubicles. On days when Clients and Customers are visiting it is important to keep all noise to a minimum in the Kitchen.
